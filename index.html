Keep your pieces special

Jewellery care guide

  • Keep jewellery away from perfumes, lotions and harsh chemicals.
  • Remove jewellery before bathing or swimming.
  • Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after use.
  • Store each piece separately in a dry jewellery pouch.
  • Avoid pulling or bending delicate chains and clasps.
